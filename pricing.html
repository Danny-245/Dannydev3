A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Have something else in mind?
Let’s talk it through ↗

How much will my project cost?

Your quote depends on the pages, features, integrations and content your project needs. Share your idea and budget range, and I’ll provide a tailored proposal with a clear scope.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the agreed scope and how ready your content is. Your proposal will include a delivery schedule and review milestones before work starts.

Are domain names and hosting included?

Domain registration, hosting and paid third-party services are discussed and itemised separately, so you know the setup and recurring costs.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. I can review your current site and scope a redesign, new features, integrations or targeted fixes based on what it needs.

How do revisions and payments work?

Your proposal sets out the included revision rounds, payment milestones and deliverables. Features or changes outside that scope are quoted before they are added.

Do you provide support after launch?

Post-launch support and ongoing maintenance can be arranged to fit your project. We’ll agree on coverage and any recurring costs in your proposal.
